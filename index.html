Work Console

A personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer.

Work Console is a private, single-user application. It helps its owner keep track of their own work: it reads the owner's mail and calendar, shows them on a local page, and drafts replies that the owner reviews and confirms before anything is sent.

It is not offered to the public, has no accounts to sign up for and no hosted service. Everything runs locally on the owner's PC.

Google data

With the owner's consent, Work Console uses Google APIs to read Gmail messages and Google Calendar events of the owner's own Google accounts, and to send a mail only when the owner confirms it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
